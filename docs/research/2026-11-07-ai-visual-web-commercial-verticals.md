## Executive findings / 核心发现

**中文：** 国内外优秀网站并非只有“3D + 黑金色”。真正有商业转化潜力的类型包括：工业设备可视化、外贸建材配置器、SaaS 免费试用和预约演示、品牌新品预售、精品酒店预订、专业服务可信度建立、创意作品集、健康医疗咨询、餐饮零售预约。AI 可以提速生成、布局和代码，但客户真正购买的是独立品牌辨识度、可信内容、可操作的业务流程和完整交付。

**English:** AI-assisted site design spans industrial engineering, direct-to-consumer hardware, B2B SaaS, boutique hospitality, creative portfolios, medical practices and local services. A production-ready premium site needs client-specific art direction and a measurable customer action, not just a fashionable hero gradient.

### Evidence hierarchy / 证据等级

- **Creator case / 原作者复盘** — declared author, links, design decisions and code/tech mechanisms (best evidence for engineering learning).
- **Creator-submitted site / 作者申报** — a company/designer reported AI used, but cannot infer the entire site was AI-only or independently validated.
- **One-prompt preview / 一键演示** — a curated website preview and its prompt, **not** an actual paying client, not proof of production availability.
- **Gallery/award entry / 索引与奖项** — confirms featured listing, not copyright permission, performance, conversion uplift or full tech stack.
- **Community reaction / 社区讨论** — qualitative feedback only, not market-size or measured conversion data.

## The AI sameness trap / AI 味道与视觉标准

Open community threads [r/webdesign, Nov 5 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/webdesign/comments/1xo8icb/if_your_landing_page_looks_aigenerated_people/) and [r/SaaS, Sep 20](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/comments/1wlcl48/anyone_else_notice_every_aibuilt_site_looks_the/) contain repeated subjective criticisms of generic prompt-generated gradient backgrounds, identical three-card sections, bland messaging and untrustworthy visuals. Those are opinions, **not measured conversion uplift or a representative sample**. Our QA should still test: project-specific art direction; real customer photos/3D assets; meaningful CTA; brand consistency; mobile and low-power performance; accessible text; clear IP.
